# Cold outreach copy — agency ops platform (pre-sell / validation)

**Sender:** Alex — alex@orbitboyzz.me
**Stage:** Pre-build. We are validating demand, not claiming a shipped product.

Being upfront that it's not built yet is a feature, not a weakness. "Would you
use this?" gets replies from people who'd never answer "buy my product." It also
means nobody feels misled when they ask for a demo.

## Required footer (CAN-SPAM — every send, no exceptions)

> OrbitBoyzz — 33 Florence Ave, Ewing, NJ 08618
> Not relevant? [Unsubscribe]({{unsubscribe_url}}) and I won't email again.

CAN-SPAM requires a working opt-out, honored within 10 business days, plus a
real physical postal address. Both are cheap to include and expensive to omit.

---

## Sending rules

- **20-30/day max.** Not 300. Volume spikes from a new domain are the single
  clearest spam signal.
- **Personal/probable-personal addresses first** — the 69 leads at the top of
  `agency_leads.csv`. Those reach a human. `info@` is the most-filtered
  inbox at any company; save it for after the copy is proven.
- **One follow-up**, 4-5 days later, then stop. Never a third.
- Plain text. No images, no tracking pixel, no link shorteners — every one of
  those hurts deliverability on a cold domain.
- Reply-to a monitored mailbox. Replies are the entire point of this campaign.

## Measuring it

Target on 30 sends: 40%+ open, 8-15% reply, 3-5% "yes let's talk."

- Under 20% open → deliverability problem, not a copy problem. Check DKIM/SPF.
- Good opens, no replies → the offer is wrong. Rewrite before sending more.

Fewer than 5 positive replies across 100 sends means the pitch isn't landing.
Change the offer rather than buying more leads.
