# Cold outreach copy — agency ops platform (pre-sell / validation)

**Sender:** Alex — alex@orbitboyzz.me
**Stage:** Pre-build. Validating demand, not claiming a shipped product.

Leading with "you came up in my research" works because it's true — these leads
came from a real geographic + service filter. It reads as specific rather than
blasted, and it survives the obvious reply of "how did you find me?"

Being upfront that it isn't built yet is a feature. "Would you use this?" gets
replies from people who'd never answer a product pitch, and nobody feels misled
when they ask for a demo.

All variants under 120 words. Merge fields: `{{first_name}}`, `{{agency_name}}`, `{{city}}`.

## How to test 5 variants properly

With 300 leads you cannot get a clean read on five things at once. Do it in waves:

1. **Wave 1 — 100 leads, 20 each.** Send A/B/C/D/E. You're looking for reply
   rate, not opens.
2. **Kill the bottom three.** With 20 sends per variant, only a gap of roughly
   3+ replies means anything. Anything tighter is noise — don't over-read it.
3. **Wave 2 — 100 leads**, top two variants, 50 each.
4. **Wave 3 — remaining 100** on the winner.

Send the **personal / probable-personal addresses first** (top 69 in
`agency_leads.csv`). Those reach a human. `info@` is the most-filtered inbox at
any company — save it for after the copy is proven.

## Sending rules

- 20-30/day max. Volume spikes are the clearest spam signal there is.
- Plain text. No images, no tracking pixels, no link shorteners.
- One follow-up at 4-5 days, then stop. Never a third.
- Reply-to a mailbox you actually watch. Replies are the whole point.

## Reading the results

Target across 30 sends: 40%+ open, 8-15% reply, 3-5% "let's talk."

- Under 20% open → deliverability, not copy. Check DKIM/SPF before touching a word.
- Good opens, no replies → the offer is wrong. Rewrite before sending more.

Fewer than 5 positive replies across 100 sends means the pitch isn't landing.
Change the offer rather than buying more leads.
